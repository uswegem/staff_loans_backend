## THE LOAN
+ {#loan-limits} The Bank lends the Employee the amount stated in the Loan Details for the period stated there. The loan amount may not exceed TZS 300,000,000 (three hundred million shillings) and the period may not exceed one hundred and twenty (120) months, subject to the Employee's affordability and the Policy.
+ The Bank shall pay the loan, less the fees deducted at disbursement and any balance of an existing staff loan being refinanced, into the Employee's salary account with the Bank.
+ Eligibility: The Employee confirms that he or she is a confirmed employee with at least six (6) months' continuous service, is not serving notice of resignation or termination, and that the information given in the loan application is true and complete.
+ {#affordability} Affordability: The Bank shall not grant a loan whose instalment, together with the Employee's other deductions, would reduce the Employee's take-home pay below the minimum set by the Policy or by law.
+ {#top-up} Top-up and new loans: Any top-up or new staff loan is made under this Agreement on new Loan Details accepted and signed electronically in the System. Where a new loan refinances an existing one, the existing balance is paid from the new loan, and the combined amount and period remain within {{ref:loan-limits}}.
## INTEREST AND FEES
+ {#interest} Interest: The loan bears interest at a fixed rate of six per cent (6%) per annum, calculated on the reducing balance from disbursement until full repayment. The total interest and total amount repayable are shown in the Loan Details.
+ Fixed rate: The rate in {{ref:interest}} does not change during the loan period, except under {{ref:commercial-terms}} when employment ends.
+ Fees: The Employee pays only the fees shown in the Loan Details, being a processing fee of not more than 1% of a new loan or 0.5% of a top-up and any credit life insurance premium the Policy requires. These may be deducted at disbursement.
## {#repayment} REPAYMENT AND SALARY DEDUCTION
+ The Employee shall repay the loan by the monthly instalments shown in the Loan Details, starting in the month shown there, until the Outstanding Balance is paid in full.
+ {#salary-deduction} Salary deduction: The Employee agrees in writing, by signing this Agreement, that the Bank as employer may deduct each instalment from his or her Salary on each pay date and apply it to the loan. This consent is given for the purposes of the Employment and Labour Relations Act, Cap. 366, and may not be withdrawn while any amount is outstanding.
+ {#debit-order} Debit order: If a deduction is not made in any month for any reason (including unpaid leave or suspension without pay), the Employee authorises the Bank as his or her banker to debit the instalment from the salary account or any other account he or she holds with the Bank, and shall otherwise pay it within seven (7) days.
+ {#arrears} Arrears: Any missed instalment is recovered from later pay dates by increasing the deduction, within the limit in {{ref:affordability}}, or by extending the loan period by agreement within {{ref:loan-limits}}. Interest continues on the reducing balance, and no penalty is charged on arrears.
+ Payments are applied first to fees and costs due, then to interest and then to principal. The Employee shall not pay instalments in cash to any employee of the Bank; payments may be made only into the loan account or the Bank's official collection account.
+ The authorities in clauses {{num:salary-deduction}} and {{num:debit-order}} may be adjusted for a top-up under {{ref:top-up}} or recovery of arrears under {{ref:arrears}}, extend to Terminal Benefits under {{ref:terminal-benefits}}, and end when the loan is fully repaid.
+ {#schedule-on-request} Repayment schedule: A repayment schedule showing each instalment and the balance after it is available to the Employee on request from the Bank's Operations or Credit department.
## EARLY SETTLEMENT
+ The Employee may repay the whole or part of the loan at any time on giving People & Culture seven (7) days' notice, without any early settlement fee, by paying the principal outstanding and interest up to the date of payment. Part payments reduce the remaining instalments or the loan period, as the Employee chooses.
## {#leaving-employment} LEAVING EMPLOYMENT
+ The Employee shall tell People & Culture in writing as soon as he or she gives or receives notice ending employment. From that time the Bank may stop further top-ups.
+ {#terminal-benefits} Terminal Benefits: The Employee agrees in writing that, when employment ends for any reason, the Bank may deduct the Outstanding Balance from his or her Terminal Benefits, to the extent permitted by law, and pay the balance of those benefits to the Employee.
+ {#commercial-terms} Conversion to commercial terms: Any amount still outstanding after {{ref:terminal-benefits}} bears interest, from the date employment ends, at the Bank's prevailing commercial personal-loan rate, which the Bank shall notify to the former Employee in writing. It is repaid by debit order or monthly payments over the remaining loan period, or another period agreed in writing within thirty (30) days after employment ends.
+ If no repayment arrangement is agreed within that thirty (30) day period, or the former Employee misses an agreed payment, {{ref:default}} applies.
## {#default} DEFAULT AND RECOVERY
+ Events of default:
(x) failure to pay an amount due under clause {{num:debit-order}} or {{num:commercial-terms}};
(x) any attempt to cancel or stop the salary deduction or debit order;
(x) false or misleading information in the loan application;
(x) bankruptcy, or a court judgment against the Employee that the Bank reasonably considers puts repayment at risk; or
(x) breach of any other term of this Agreement that is not remedied within fourteen (14) days of notice.
+ Remedies: If an event of default occurs, the Bank may give the Employee fourteen (14) days' written notice to remedy it. If it is not remedied, the Bank may declare the Outstanding Balance immediately due and recover it by any lawful means, including legal action.
+ Set-off: After giving seven (7) days' written notice, the Bank may apply any credit balance in the Employee's accounts with the Bank to an amount that is due and unpaid.
+ Costs: The Employee shall pay the reasonable legal and collection costs actually incurred by the Bank in recovering an amount in default.
+ Misconduct: Misuse of the loan, false information or any arrangement to defeat the deductions may also be dealt with under the Bank's disciplinary code. Disciplinary action does not affect the Employee's obligation to repay.
+ Discharge: Within fourteen (14) days after the Outstanding Balance is paid in full, the Bank shall stop the deductions, refund any amount received in excess of the amount due and, on request, issue a clearance letter.
