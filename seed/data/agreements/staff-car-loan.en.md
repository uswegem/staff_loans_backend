## 1. DEFINITIONS
1.1 In this Agreement:
- "Loan" means the amount lent under the Loan Details and, where the context requires, the amount outstanding from time to time;
- "Vehicle" means the motor vehicle shown in the Loan Details, including its registration card, keys, accessories and any insurance proceeds relating to it;
- "Loan Details" means the details of the Employee's loan generated by the System and shown to the Employee before signing, including the Employee's name and personal details, the loan amount, period, interest rate, monthly instalment, fees, total interest, total amount repayable and deduction dates, and, for a car loan, the vehicle and seller details. The Loan Details form part of this Agreement;
- "Outstanding Balance" means the principal, interest, fees and other amounts owed under this Agreement at any time;
- "Policy" means the Bank's Staff Loans Policy in force from time to time;
- "Salary" means the Employee's monthly salary and allowances paid by the Bank;
- "System" means the Bank's HR, loan origination or electronic signature system through which the loan is applied for, approved and signed; and
- "Terminal Benefits" means amounts payable by the Bank to the Employee when employment ends, such as final salary, leave pay, notice pay and bonus, but not benefits payable by a social security fund.
## 2. THE LOAN
2.1 The Bank lends the Employee the amount stated in the Loan Details, only to buy the Vehicle for his or her personal use. The loan amount may not exceed TZS 200,000,000 (two hundred million shillings) and the period may not exceed sixty (60) months (five years), subject to the Employee's affordability and the Policy.
2.2 Eligibility: The Employee confirms that he or she is a confirmed employee with at least six (6) months' continuous service, is not serving notice of resignation or termination, and that the information given in the application is true and complete.
2.3 Disbursement: The Bank shall pay the loan directly to the seller or dealer shown in the Loan Details, after it has verified the seller and the Vehicle and, where it requires, obtained a valuation and inspection. The loan is never paid to the Employee. The Employee pays his or her own contribution (if any) to the seller before disbursement.
2.4 Before disbursement the Bank must also receive the seller's invoice and the Vehicle's registration documents, the signed TRA transfer documents needed for clause 5.1 and a comprehensive insurance cover note under clause 6.
## 3. INTEREST, FEES AND REPAYMENT
3.1 Interest: The loan bears interest at a fixed rate of six per cent (6%) per annum, calculated on the reducing balance from disbursement until full repayment. The total interest and total amount repayable are shown in the Loan Details.
3.2 Fixed rate: The rate in clause 3.1 does not change during the loan period, except under clause 7.2 when employment ends.
3.3 Fees and costs: The Employee pays only the fees shown in the Loan Details, the insurance premium under clause 6 and the actual costs of TRA registration and of registering and releasing the Bank's security.
3.4 Repayment: The Employee shall repay the loan by the equal monthly instalments shown in the Loan Details, starting in the month shown there, until the Outstanding Balance is paid in full.
3.5 Salary deduction: The Employee agrees in writing, by signing this Agreement, that the Bank as employer may deduct each instalment and the annual insurance premium from his or her Salary on each pay date. This consent is given for the purposes of the Employment and Labour Relations Act, Cap. 366, and may not be withdrawn while any amount is outstanding. Any amount not deducted may be debited from the Employee's accounts with the Bank, and missed amounts are recovered from later pay dates within the take-home pay limit set by the Policy or by law. This authority ends when the loan is fully repaid.
3.6 Early settlement: The Employee may repay the whole or part of the loan at any time on seven (7) days' notice, without any early settlement fee, by paying the principal outstanding and interest up to the date of payment.
## 4. USE AND CARE OF THE VEHICLE
4.1 Until the Outstanding Balance is paid in full, the Employee shall: (a) keep the Vehicle roadworthy, serviced and in his or her possession in Tanzania; (b) use it lawfully and not for hire or commercial purposes without the Bank's written consent; (c) pay all licences, taxes and fines on it; (d) allow the Bank to inspect it on reasonable notice; and (e) report any accident, theft, seizure or serious damage to the Bank within forty-eight (48) hours.
## 5. SECURITY
5.1 Joint registration: The Vehicle shall be registered with the Tanzania Revenue Authority in the joint names of the Bank and the Employee, and the Bank shall keep the original registration card until the Outstanding Balance is paid in full.
5.2 Security interest: The Employee grants the Bank a first-ranking security interest over the Vehicle to secure the Outstanding Balance, and authorises the Bank to register it in the collateral registry under the Movable Property Security Rights Act, 2019.
5.3 No dealing: The Employee shall not sell, transfer, lease, pledge or otherwise dispose of or encumber the Vehicle, or remove the Bank's name from the registration records, until the Outstanding Balance is paid in full.
5.4 Release: Within fourteen (14) days after the Outstanding Balance is paid in full, the Bank shall return the registration card, sign the TRA documents to remove its name, discharge the registration under clause 5.2, stop the deductions and refund any amount received in excess of the amount due.
## 6. INSURANCE
6.1 The Vehicle shall be comprehensively insured for its full value throughout the loan period through the Bank's bancassurance arrangement with an approved insurer, with the Bank noted as first loss payee. The Employee pays the premium, which is recovered under clause 3.5.
6.2 If the insurance lapses, the Bank may renew it and recover the cost from the Employee. Insurance proceeds for loss or total loss of the Vehicle are paid to the Bank and applied to the Outstanding Balance, and any surplus is paid to the Employee within fourteen (14) days. Proceeds for repairable damage are used to repair the Vehicle.
## 7. LEAVING EMPLOYMENT
7.1 The Employee shall tell People & Culture in writing as soon as he or she gives or receives notice ending employment. The Employee agrees in writing that, when employment ends for any reason, the Bank may deduct the Outstanding Balance from his or her Terminal Benefits, to the extent permitted by law.
7.2 Any amount still outstanding bears interest, from the date employment ends, at the Bank's prevailing commercial vehicle-loan rate, which the Bank shall notify to the former Employee in writing. It is repaid by debit order or monthly payments over the remaining loan period, or another period agreed in writing within thirty (30) days after employment ends. The security in clause 5 and the insurance in clause 6 continue until full repayment.
## 8. DEFAULT AND ENFORCEMENT
8.1 Events of default: (a) failure to pay an amount due under clause 3.5 or 7.2; (b) any attempt to cancel the salary deduction or debit order; (c) any dealing with the Vehicle in breach of clause 5.3, or failure to keep it insured; (d) false information, including any arrangement to inflate the purchase price, obtain a refund or rebate from the seller or divert any part of the loan; (e) bankruptcy, or seizure of the Vehicle by a third party; or (f) breach of any other term not remedied within fourteen (14) days of notice.
8.2 Remedies: If an event of default occurs, the Bank may give the Employee fourteen (14) days' written notice to remedy it. If it is not remedied, the Bank may declare the Outstanding Balance immediately due and, subject to the notices required by the Movable Property Security Rights Act, 2019, take possession of the Vehicle through a licensed auctioneer or court broker and sell it at the best price reasonably obtainable.
8.3 Sale proceeds are applied to the reasonable costs of repossession and sale and then to the Outstanding Balance. Any surplus is paid to the Employee within fourteen (14) days, and the Employee remains liable for any shortfall.
8.4 Set-off and costs: After seven (7) days' written notice, the Bank may apply any credit balance in the Employee's accounts with the Bank to an amount due and unpaid. The Employee shall pay the reasonable legal and recovery costs actually incurred.
8.5 Misconduct: Any matter in clause 8.1(d) is also a breach of the Policy and may be dealt with under the Bank's disciplinary code. Disciplinary action does not affect the Employee's obligation to repay.
